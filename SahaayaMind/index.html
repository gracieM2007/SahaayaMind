💡 How SahaayaMind Works (In Brief)

🧠 Purpose & Cognitive Support

Developed by Team Arbalest for SIH 2026. SahaayaMind is specifically tailored for elderly individuals with neurological or cognitive difficulties (such as memory loss, mild cognitive impairment, or attention challenges). It delivers gentle, pressure-free daily mental exercises to stimulate cognitive pathways and boost mental sharpness.

🔒 Zero Login • 100% Local Device Memory

No logins, accounts, or personal passwords required. All activity data, progress statistics, and daily streaks are recorded continuously and permanently right on this device without collecting personal information.

📊 3-Factor Overall Performance Formula

After every activity, your overall performance score (0–100) is dynamically calculated using three core cognitive factors:

  • 🎯 Accuracy (45% weight): Higher accuracy directly elevates your score, rewarding careful and deliberate choices.
  • ⚡ Attempt Efficiency (30% weight): Minimizing unnecessary attempts reflects focused recall and higher confidence.
  • ⏱️ Time Pacing (25% weight): Calm, steady completion without taking too long or stressing the senior.

🔥 Continuous Device Streak

Playing just 1 short session every day extends your daily streak forever. Consistency stimulates brain neuroplasticity and builds lasting cognitive stamina.

🗣️ 5-Language Voice Companion (Google Gemini AI)

Powered by Google Gemini AI with native voice and text support in Hindi, Marathi, English, Assamese, and Bodo, ensuring comfortable access for regional seniors.
