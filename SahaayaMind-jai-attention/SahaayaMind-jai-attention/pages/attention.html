Attention Exercise

Find and select the matching objects. It is a gentle focus workout — there is no timer, so take your time.

How to play

  1. 1 Read the instruction, for example “Select all the 🍎 apples”.
  2. 2 Tap every object that matches. Tap it again to unselect it.
  3. 3 Press Check Answers — the round shows what you found and missed.
  4. 4 Finish 3 rounds to see your score, result and progress.
